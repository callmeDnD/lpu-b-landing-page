Our Story

Lyceum of the Philippines University – Batangas

LPU Batangas is a campus of the Lyceum of the Philippines University system, serving students from Batangas and nearby provinces. Guided by the motto Pro Deo et Patria (For God and Country), the university forms graduates who are competent, ethical and ready to contribute to society.

Mission

To provide quality, relevant and values-based education that develops competent professionals and responsible citizens.


Vision

To be a leading university recognized for academic excellence, innovation and service to community and country.
